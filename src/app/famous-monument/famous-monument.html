Monument of Eternal Glory (Park fame), Zhytomyr

Eternal fire and Monument of Glory. Monument of Eternal Glory opened May 9, 1979. The authors of the monument - sculptor Kolomyjec and Husid, architects Ignashchenko and Ivanov. The monument is a massive high cylindrical column, carved out of Zhytomyr labradorite on which the sculpture - the Soviet Army, partisans and female patriot. Above them flies the flag. The sculptural cast in bronze. On the granite pedestal engraved name of the Soviet Army, partisan units and detachments underground organizations, the names of warlords, guerrilla commanders, leaders of the party underground. Near the monument burns the eternal flame. The complex, a monument of eternal glory, eternal flame and park rides make Park of Fame, which is located on the picturesque banks river Teteriv in Zhytomyr (str. Chudnivsk 51-69).
